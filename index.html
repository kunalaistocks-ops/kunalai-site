WORD OF THE DAY

Edge AI

Edge AI means running AI on the device itself, like a phone, camera or satellite, instead of sending data to a faraway data centre. It can be faster and use less bandwidth.

MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. Myth or Fact: An AI tool always tells you when it is unsure, so a confident answer is a correct answer.

    Myth. AI tools can sound confident while being wrong. Always check important facts, numbers and names before you use them.

  2. Myth or Fact: A realistic AI-generated image of a person can be made without that person ever posing for a photo.

    Fact. Image tools can create lifelike pictures from text or from other photos. That is why fake images can spread easily and need checking.

  3. Myth or Fact: Only large tech companies can use AI in their daily work.

    Myth. Many small firms use AI tools every day for writing, support and analysis. You do not need a big tech team to start.

  4. Myth or Fact: Business Standard reports that two-thirds of MSMEs use AI, but only 21% deploy business software, according to a study.

    Fact. This restates the Business Standard headline. It suggests many small firms try AI before they adopt wider business software.

  5. Myth or Fact: If you give an AI tool a clearer, more detailed prompt, you usually get a better answer.

    Fact. AI works from the instructions you give it. Clear context, a goal and a format usually lead to more useful results.
