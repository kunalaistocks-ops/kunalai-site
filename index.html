THE METHOD · THE HUMAN LINE

Draw the line, task by task.

Every task sits somewhere between all-AI and all-human. Score it 0 to 2 on five questions, add them up, and it lands in one of three zones.

  1. ReversibilityCan it be undone if it's wrong?
  2. VerificationCan you check it quickly?
  3. ConfidentialityIs it sensitive?
  4. JudgmentDoes it need taste?
  5. RelationshipsWho is affected if it's wrong?

MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. If an AI tool sounds very confident, its answer is almost certainly correct.

    Myth. AI can write wrong answers in a confident tone. Always check important facts, numbers and names before you use them.

  2. SMEStreet reports that TermSheet Capital sees rising global demand for Indian AI firms.

    Fact. This is what the SMEStreet headline says. It points to growing interest in AI companies built in India.

  3. TradingView reports that AI is forcing recruitment firms to overhaul their business models.

    Fact. This restates the TradingView headline. It shows AI is changing how whole businesses work, not only single tasks.

  4. You need to know coding to get useful results from everyday AI tools.

    Myth. Most AI tools work with plain language. You type what you need in normal words, like briefing a colleague.

  5. Giving an AI tool clear context in your prompt usually leads to a better answer.

    Fact. Details like your role, your audience and the format you want help the tool understand the task and respond better.
