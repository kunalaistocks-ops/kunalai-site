WORD OF THE DAY

Physical AI

AI that senses and acts in the real world through robots or machines, not just on a screen. It combines sensors, cameras and motors with AI software.

MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. Rediff reports that IIT Kharagpur is setting up a new Physical AI school.

    Fact. This matches the headline from Rediff about IIT Kharagpur's new Physical AI school.

  2. The Economic Times reports that Nikhil Kamath asked Silicon Valley founders if the AI boom is real or a bubble.

    Fact. This directly restates the Economic Times headline about Kamath's question to founders.

  3. AI systems can think and feel emotions the way humans do.

    Myth. AI predicts patterns in data. It has no feelings, awareness or inner experience, no matter how natural it sounds.

  4. AI tools can sometimes give confident but completely false answers.

    Fact. This is called hallucination. It happens because AI predicts likely words, not verified facts.

  5. You must know how to code to use most AI tools at work today.

    Myth. Most workplace AI tools today use plain language chat or simple menus, no coding needed.
