MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. Pasting confidential company data into any free AI tool is always safe.

    Myth. Check each tool's data policy. Business versions usually offer stronger protection.

  2. A computer beat the reigning world chess champion before the year 2000.

    Fact. IBM's Deep Blue beat Garry Kasparov in 1997.

  3. AI detectors can reliably tell whether text was written by AI.

    Myth. They produce many false positives. Never use one alone to judge someone's work.

  4. Asking AI to work step by step can improve its answers on hard problems.

    Fact. Breaking a problem into steps helps models make fewer mistakes.

  5. AI usually replaces whole jobs overnight.

    Myth. It tends to take over tasks first. Roles change shape over years.
