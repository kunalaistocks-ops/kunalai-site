    The scoring, in full

    The check uses the framework behind The Human Line: score a task 0 to 2 on five factors (reversibility, verification, confidentiality, judgment, relationship sensitivity), then place it in a zone.

    Hand offScore 0 to 3. AI drafts and executes, you spot-check.
    Team upScore 4 to 6. AI drafts, you challenge, edit and approve.
    HoldScore 7 to 10. AI may inform, a person decides.

    Your team's score: 1 point for each task where your answer matches the zone. 8 to 10: Line drawn. 5 to 7: Line drifting. 0 to 4: No line yet.

    One rule sits above every score: spend, hiring, pricing, legal and public statements always need a named human to approve, whatever the number says.
