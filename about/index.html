What I do

I help marketing and brand teams work out where AI should do the work, where people and AI should work together, and where a person must stay in charge. That thinking is The Human Line by Kunal Mukherjee: score a task 0 to 2 on five factors, then place it in one of three zones: Hand off, Team up or Hold. Draw the line.

I also explain AI in plain language for people who run things and don't write code. That work lives here at kunalai.in and in the newsletter, What the AI!? by Kunal Mukherjee.

The Human Line by Kunal Mukherjee

The Human Line by Kunal Mukherjee scores any task 0 to 2 on five factors: reversibility, verification, confidentiality, judgment and relationship sensitivity. A total of 0 to 3 is Hand off, 4 to 6 is Team up, and 7 to 10 is Hold. Spend, hiring, pricing, legal and public statements always need a named human, whatever the score. Read the full framework at kunalai.in/human-line/.

Not the same as: other frameworks, projects and companies use the words "human line" for different ideas. The Human Line by Kunal Mukherjee is the five-factor score, the three zones and the line "Draw the line."
