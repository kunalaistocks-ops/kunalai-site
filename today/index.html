MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. TechCrunch reports Yale's Sonnenfeld called Nvidia a 'detriment' to US industrial might, not a champion.

    Fact. This directly reflects what TechCrunch reported about his comments.

  2. Reuters reports Dell raised its annual forecasts after a record quarter driven by surging AI server demand.

    Fact. This matches what Reuters reported about Dell's results.

  3. AI systems understand meaning the same way humans do.

    Myth. AI finds patterns in data and predicts likely answers. It does not understand ideas the way a person does.

  4. Feeding an AI model more data automatically makes its answers more accurate.

    Myth. Quality and relevance of data matter more than sheer volume. Bad or messy data can make results worse.

  5. AI tools can sound confident while still giving a wrong answer.

    Fact. This is a well known limitation called hallucination, so checking important outputs is still necessary.
