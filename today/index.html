MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. Bloomberg reports that India is emerging as an AI haven seeking a boost from new US chip curbs.

    Fact. This is what the Bloomberg headline states directly.

  2. News On AIR reports that India, the US and eight other nations signed a declaration to safeguard AI supply chains.

    Fact. This matches the News On AIR headline exactly.

  3. AI systems genuinely understand meaning the way a human mind does.

    Myth. AI finds patterns in data and predicts likely outputs, it does not understand in the human sense.

  4. You must know how to code to use most AI tools at work today.

    Myth. Most modern AI tools are built for plain language instructions, no coding needed.

  5. AI tools can sound confident while giving an answer that is completely wrong.

    Fact. AI models predict likely words, so fluent and wrong answers can look identical to fluent and right ones.
