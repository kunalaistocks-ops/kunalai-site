WORD OF THE DAY

Key person risk

This is the danger a company faces when it depends heavily on one leader or expert. If that person leaves or is lost, the business can struggle to function.

TRY THIS PROMPT TODAY

FOR LEADERSHIP

List the top 5 risks my business faces if we depend too heavily on [one vendor, leader, or AI tool] for [critical function]. Suggest ways to reduce this dependency.

MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. TechCrunch reports S&P is considering a credit downgrade for Nvidia over governance and key man risks.

    Fact. This directly restates what TechCrunch reported about S&P's review of Nvidia's credit standing.

  2. News On AIR reports that India, the U.S. and eight other nations signed a declaration to safeguard AI supply chains.

    Fact. This matches the headline about ten nations agreeing to protect AI supply chains.

  3. AI models always give the exact same answer to the same question every time.

    Myth. Many AI tools include randomness by design, so the same question can produce slightly different answers.

  4. AI tools generally improve when they are trained on larger amounts of relevant data.

    Fact. More good quality data usually helps AI systems recognise patterns more accurately.

  5. AI can fully replace human judgment in every business decision without any oversight.

    Myth. AI can support decisions but lacks context, accountability and ethical judgment that humans provide.
