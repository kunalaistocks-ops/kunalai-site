WORD OF THE DAY

Zero data retention

Zero data retention means an AI provider processes your data but does not store it afterwards, so nothing sits on their servers later.

TRY THIS PROMPT TODAY

FOR LEGAL

Review our contract with [AI vendor name] and list exactly what it says about storing, retaining or deleting our company data.

MYTH OR FACT? · FIVE NEW QUESTIONS EVERY DAY

  1. Business Standard reports that Anthropic introduced a zero data retention option for firms using advanced AI.

    Fact. This is what the headline states about Anthropic's new offering for business users.

  2. ET Datacenters reports that India tops a digital sovereignty index but struggles with AI control.

    Fact. This directly reflects what the headline says about India's ranking and challenge.

  3. AI models understand language the same way humans do, with real comprehension and feelings.

    Myth. AI predicts likely word patterns from data. It has no understanding, feelings or awareness.

  4. AI tools can sound completely confident while giving an answer that is factually wrong.

    Fact. This is a well known limitation. Confidence in tone never guarantees accuracy, so checking matters.

  5. Once an AI model is trained, it never needs to be updated or retrained again.

    Myth. Models can go stale as information changes, so providers regularly retrain or update them.
